# AI Policy

Using AI tools (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT...) to contribute to Elysia is allowed. The rules below keep reviews fair to maintainers' time.

## Rules

- **Disclose it.** Say in the PR or issue that AI was used and roughly how (autocomplete, generated code, wrote the description...).
- **You are the author.** You must understand every line you submit and be able to explain and defend it in review. "The AI wrote it" is not an answer.
- **Test it yourself.** Run the code and the checks before opening the PR. Do not submit untested changes.
- **Keep it small.** Large AI-generated PRs that were not discussed first will likely be closed.
- **Write your own words in discussions.** Issues, reviews and comments should come from you. AI may help with wording or translation, but don't paste walls of generated text.
- **No AI-generated bug or security reports** that you haven't verified and reproduced yourself.
